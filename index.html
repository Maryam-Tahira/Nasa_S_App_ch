📊 NASA Open Datasets

Real datasets from NASA — free to use for research, analysis, and space exploration projects

🌍 Earth Observation

NASA Earthdata FREE

The gateway to NASA's entire Earth science data collection — atmospheric, oceanic, land, cryosphere, and human dimensions data from dozens of satellite missions (Terra, Aqua, Landsat, ICESat-2, GRACE-FO).

earthdata.nasa.gov ↗

NASA POWER REST API FREE

Worldwide meteorological data — solar radiation, temperature, humidity, wind speed, precipitation. Derived from satellite observations. Perfect for renewable energy and agriculture research.

power.larc.nasa.gov ↗

FIRMS — Fire Information for Resource Management FREE

Near real-time active fire data from MODIS and VIIRS satellites. Global fire detection within 3 hours. Used by firefighters, researchers, and disaster management worldwide.

firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov ↗

Giovanni — Geospatial Interactive Online Visualization FREE

Analyze and visualize Earth science data without downloading — temperature maps, rainfall patterns, aerosol distributions, ocean chlorophyll. Browser-based analysis tool.

giovanni.gsfc.nasa.gov ↗

🪐 Planetary Science

Planetary Data System (PDS) FREE

NASA's official archive for all planetary mission data — Mars rovers, Cassini, Juno, New Horizons, MESSENGER, Dawn. Surface images, spectra, radar, gravity maps of every visited body.

pds.nasa.gov ↗

NASA Open APIs REST API FREE KEY

APOD (daily astronomy picture), Mars Rover Photos (Perseverance, Curiosity), NEO (near-Earth objects), DONKI (space weather), EPIC (Earth imagery from DSCOVR), Exoplanet Archive, TechTransfer.

api.nasa.gov ↗ — Free API key, 1000 requests/hour

Planetary Fact Sheets FREE

Authoritative comparison tables for all planets and major moons — mass, radius, density, gravity, escape velocity, orbital parameters, atmosphere composition, magnetic field, temperature ranges.

nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet ↗

JPL Solar System Dynamics API FREE

Precise orbital elements, ephemerides, and physical parameters for all solar system objects — planets, moons, asteroids, comets. Horizons API for spacecraft trajectory data.

ssd.jpl.nasa.gov ↗

🌌 Astronomy & Astrophysics

NASA Exoplanet Archive API FREE

Complete catalog of 5,000+ confirmed exoplanets — orbital parameters, mass, radius, host star properties, discovery method. TAP query service for programmatic access.

exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu ↗

MAST — Mikulski Archive for Space Telescopes FREE

All data from Hubble, JWST, Kepler/K2, TESS, GALEX — astronomical images, spectra, light curves. Billions of observations spanning the entire electromagnetic spectrum.

mast.stsci.edu ↗

HEASARC — High Energy Astrophysics Archive FREE

X-ray and gamma-ray astronomy data from Chandra, Swift, Fermi, NICER, NuSTAR. Black holes, neutron stars, active galaxies, gamma-ray bursts, cosmic X-ray background.

heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov ↗

🛰️ Space Technology & Imagery

NASA Image and Video Library API FREE

Search and download from NASA's vast public-domain archive — 140,000+ images, 14,000+ videos. Hubble, JWST, Apollo, Mars rovers, ISS, launches, astronaut portraits.

images.nasa.gov ↗

NASA Open Data Portal FREE

Central portal for all NASA open datasets — 40,000+ datasets covering climate, aeronautics, space science, software, patents, facilities. Machine-readable formats (CSV, JSON, XML).

data.nasa.gov ↗

NASA Open Source Software FREE

Open source code from NASA — flight software, simulation tools, data analysis, visualization, mission planning. Available for reuse and contribution.

code.nasa.gov ↗

TechPort — NASA Technology Portfolio API FREE

Database of NASA's active and completed technology projects — 2,500+ projects, technology readiness levels, descriptions, responsible centers, status updates.

techport.nasa.gov ↗

🌍 Space Centers of the World

🇺🇸

Kennedy Space Center

Cape Canaveral, Florida — NASA's primary launch site

Launch Complex 39 — where Apollo missions and Space Shuttles launched. Now hosts SLS/Artemis, SpaceX Falcon 9/Heavy, and Boeing Starliner. Visitor Complex receives 1.5M+ visitors/year.
🇰🇿

Baikonur Cosmodrome

Kazakhstan — world's first and largest space launch facility

Operational since 1957. Sputnik 1 and Yuri Gagarin launched from here. Still primary launch site for Russian Soyuz missions to ISS. Leased by Russia from Kazakhstan.
🇫🇷

Guiana Space Centre

Kourou, French Guiana — ESA's primary launch site

Near equator (5°N) — ideal for geostationary orbits. Ariane 5/6, Vega rockets. JWST launched from here December 2021. Europe's gateway to space.
🇨🇳

Wenchang Space Center

Hainan Island, China — newest Chinese launch facility

Operational since 2016. Launches Long March 5/7/8. China's space station modules, lunar missions (Chang'e), and Mars missions (Tianwen) launch from here.
🇮🇳

Satish Dhawan Space Centre

Sriharikota, India — ISRO's primary launch center

PSLV and GSLV launches. Chandrayaan missions to Moon. Mangalyaan (Mars orbiter). Gaganyaan (human spaceflight) planned. India's space program hub.
🇯🇵

Tanegashima Space Center

Tanegashima Island, Japan — JAXA's primary launch site

H-IIA/H3 rocket launches. Known as "most beautiful launch site." Hayabusa asteroid sample return missions. SLIM lunar lander (2024).

🚀 Active Spacecraft Missions

🛸

Voyager 1

Farthest human-made object — 24+ billion km from Earth

Launched 1977. Entered interstellar space 2012. Still sending data at 160 bits/sec. Golden Record carries sounds of Earth. Power from plutonium RTG — will last until ~2025.
🛸

Voyager 2

Only spacecraft to visit Uranus and Neptune

Launched 1977 (before Voyager 1!). Grand Tour of Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune. Entered interstellar space 2018. ~20 billion km from Earth.
🔴

Perseverance + Ingenuity

Active on Mars since Feb 2021

Jezero Crater — ancient lake bed. Searching for biosignatures. Ingenuity: first powered flight on another planet (72+ flights!). MOXIE: CO₂→O₂ conversion demonstrated.
🪐

New Horizons

First Pluto flyby (2015), now in Kuiper Belt

Revealed Pluto's heart-shaped nitrogen ice plain (Sputnik Planitia). Flew past Arrokoth (2019) — most distant object ever visited. Now 8+ billion km from Earth.
☀️

Parker Solar Probe

Closest-ever approach to the Sun — touching the corona

Launched 2018. Reached 635,266 km/h — fastest human-made object ever. Entered Sun's corona (atmosphere) in 2021. Heat shield withstands 1,377°C.
🌌

JWST

Most powerful telescope ever — L2 orbit, 1.5M km from Earth

6.5m gold-coated mirror. Infrared vision sees first galaxies (13+ billion years old). Analyzing exoplanet atmospheres for biosignatures. $10 billion, 25+ years to build.

🏗️ NASA Rocket History & Current Programs

🚀

Saturn V (1967-1973)

Apollo Moon missions · 13 launches · 100% success

Height: 110.6m. The most powerful rocket ever flown until SLS. Carried Apollo astronauts to the Moon. 7.5M lbs thrust. Each launch cost ~$1.16 billion (today's dollars). 12 humans walked on the Moon using this rocket.
🛸

Space Shuttle (1981-2011)

135 missions · ISS construction · Hubble servicing

First reusable spacecraft. 5 orbiters: Columbia, Challenger, Discovery, Atlantis, Endeavour. Built the ISS. Deployed and serviced Hubble. Carried 355 people to space. Columbia (2003) and Challenger (1986) were lost.
🔥

SLS — Space Launch System (Active)

Current · Artemis program · Most powerful rocket flying

Height: 98m. 8.8M lbs thrust (15% more than Saturn V). Artemis I completed Nov 2022 (uncrewed). Artemis II (crewed lunar flyby) and Artemis III (lunar landing) upcoming. Uses RS-25 engines from Space Shuttle program.
🏗️

Artemis IV & Beyond (In Development)

Building now · Block 2 SLS · Deep space missions

SLS Block 2 will be most powerful rocket ever built. Exploration Upper Stage (EUS) in development at Boeing. Will launch Gateway modules and eventually support Mars missions. Block 1B (upgraded) comes first, then Block 2.
⚡

SpaceX Falcon 9 / Heavy

NASA's primary crew launcher · 300+ launches · Reusable

Falcon 9: 70m, reusable first stage, 200+ successful landings. Falcon Heavy: 3 cores, most powerful operational rocket (before SLS). Carries NASA astronauts to ISS via Crew Dragon. Starship (next-gen) selected as Artemis lunar lander.
🌟

SpaceX Starship (Testing)

Next-gen · Full reusability · Mars capable

Height: 121m (tallest ever). Both stages reusable. 16.7M lbs thrust (2× Saturn V). Selected by NASA as Artemis HLS lunar lander. Multiple test flights completed. Goal: make humans multiplanetary.
